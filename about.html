About The Project

Basic information about the website idea and its users.

Home About Booking Contact Us

Main Objective

To provide a simple and reliable website that helps promote Uganda's tourism and makes it easier for users to access information and basic booking support.

Target Audience

  • International tourists
  • Local tourists
  • Travel planners and agencies
  • Students and researchers

Important Pages

  1. Home page for background and key tourism information
  2. About page for the objective and target audience
  3. Booking page for simple booking requests
  4. Contact page for communication and support

Expected Benefits

  • Better visibility of Uganda's tourist attractions
  • Simple access to travel and booking support
  • Improved awareness of local tourism services
