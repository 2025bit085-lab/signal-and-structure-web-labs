Basic information about the website idea and its users.

Main Objective

To provide a simple and reliable website that helps promote Uganda's tourism and makes it easier for users to access information and basic booking support.

Target Audience

  • International tourists
  • Local tourists
  • Travel planners and agencies
  • Students and researchers

Important Pages

The project uses four simple pages: Home, About, Booking, and Contact Us.
