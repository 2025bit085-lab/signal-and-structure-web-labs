Pearl Trails Uganda

A simple student website project about promoting tourism in Uganda.

Background

Uganda has many tourist attractions, cultural places, and natural experiences. However, many visitors may not easily find all the information they need in one place.

Where We Are Lacking

  • Tourism information is scattered across different sources.
  • Some visitors may struggle to find trusted services and booking details.
  • Small tourism businesses may not be visible enough online.

What This Website Tries To Do

This website is meant to provide one simple platform where users can read about attractions, view services, send booking requests, and contact the tourism team.
